# Software Engineering Reading

This is a separate reading section for software-engineering principles that are
useful alongside MCA study but are not part of a specific semester subject.

## Featured article

- [The 56 Laws of Software Engineering](Software-Engineering-56-Laws-Combined-Article.md) - practical principles for software systems, teams, planning, quality, scale, design, and engineering decisions.

The original laws were created by Dr. Milan Milanović. The linked Markdown
article is an independent study adaptation by Shreyas Khamkar.
