# Human Rights — Cheat Sheet

## Core values

| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Dignity | Foundation of all values; respect for every individual |
| Liberty | Freedom to regulate one's own relations; basis of rights |
| Equality | Equal treatment without discrimination; concessions for disadvantaged |
| Justice | Fair and equitable treatment; highest value (Plato) |
| Ethics | Social code of conduct; distinguishes right from wrong |
| Morals | Personal character of an individual |

## Types of rights

| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| Natural | Inherent (life, liberty) |
| Legal | Created by State |
| Claim | Entitlement requiring action (right to education) |
| Liberty | Freedom from interference (freedom of speech) |
| Positive | Require provision (education, health) |
| Negative | Require non-interference (freedom from torture) |
| Individual | Held by persons |
| Group | Held by communities (minority rights) |
| Universal | Applicable everywhere |

## Types of duties

| Type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Natural | Derived from nature/conscience |
| Acquired | Arise from social role |
| Positive | Require action |
| Negative | Require abstention |
| Perfect | Always binding |
| Imperfect | Circumstance-dependent |
| Prima facie | Appear binding until overridden |

## Legal instruments

| Instrument | Binding? |
|---|---|
| Treaty | Yes |
| Convention | Yes (multilateral treaty) |
| Covenant | Yes (binding treaty) |
| Declaration | No (soft law) |
| Protocol | Yes (supplementary to treaty) |
| Charter | Yes (foundational treaty) |
| Statute | Yes (institutional law) |
| Executive order | Depends on domestic law |
| Resolution | Usually no |

## UN bodies relevant to human rights

| Body | Role |
|---|---|
| General Assembly | Deliberative; adopts declarations and conventions |
| Security Council | Can sanction for gross violations |
| ECOSOC | Coordinates human rights; oversees treaty bodies |
| Human Rights Council | Promotes and protects human rights (replaced Commission, 2006) |
| OHCHR | Leads UN human rights efforts |
| ICJ | Adjudicates disputes between States |

## UDHR articles — three generations

| Generation | Articles | Rights |
|---|---|---|
| First | 3–21 | Civil and political rights |
| Second | 22–27 | Economic, social, and cultural rights |
| Third | 28–30 | Solidarity rights (social order, duties, no abuse of rights) |

## Fundamental Duties (Article 51A)

1. Abide by the Constitution and respect its ideals and institutions.
2. Cherish the noble ideals of the national struggle for freedom.
3. Uphold and protect the sovereignty, unity, and integrity of India.
4. Defend the country and render national service when called upon.
5. Promote harmony and spirit of common brotherhood; renounce practices derogatory to the dignity of women.
6. Value and preserve the rich heritage of our composite culture.
7. Protect and improve the natural environment and have compassion for living creatures.
8. Develop the scientific temper, humanism, and the spirit of inquiry and reform.
9. Safeguard public property and abjure violence.
10. Strive towards excellence in all spheres.
11. Parent/guardian to provide education to child/ward between 6 and 14 years. *(86th Amendment, 2002)*
