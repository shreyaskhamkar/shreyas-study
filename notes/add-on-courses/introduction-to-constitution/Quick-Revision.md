# Introduction to Constitution — Quick Revision

## Module 1: Preamble

- Authority: "We, the People of India."
- Keywords: Sovereign, Socialist, Secular, Democratic, Republic.
- Objectives: Justice, Liberty, Equality, Fraternity.
- Adopted: 26 November 1949; in force: 26 January 1950.
- Socialist and Secular added by 42nd Amendment (1976).
- Part of the Constitution (Kesavananda Bharati, 1973).

## Module 2: Fundamental Rights

- FRs: justiciable, Articles 12–35.
- Right to Equality: Arts 14–18.
- Right to Freedom: Arts 19–22.
- Right against Exploitation: Arts 23–24.
- Right to Freedom of Religion: Arts 25–28.
- Cultural and Educational Rights: Arts 29–30.
- Right to Constitutional Remedies: Arts 32–35.
- Art 32 = five writs (heart and soul): habeas corpus, mandamus, prohibition, quo warranto, certiorari.
- Art 21 = life and personal liberty (judicially expanded).
- Art 21A = right to education (6–14 years).
- Art 24 = no child labour below 14 in factories/mines/hazardous employment.
- Art 30 = minorities establish and administer educational institutions.

## Module 3: Directive Principles

- DP: non-justiciable, Part IV, Articles 36–51.
- Art 38 = social order; Art 39 = policy principles (a–f).
- Art 39A = equal justice and free legal aid.
- Art 40 = village panchayats.
- Art 44 = Uniform Civil Code (not yet implemented).
- Art 46 = SC/ST/weaker sections.
- Art 48 = agriculture and animal husbandry; Art 48A = environment.
- Art 50 = separation of judiciary from executive.
- Art 51 = international peace and security.

## Module 4: Fundamental Duties

- Art 51A = 11 Fundamental Duties, Part IVA.
- Added by 42nd Amendment (1976) — 10 duties.
- 11th duty added by 86th Amendment (2002) — parent/guardian to provide education to children aged 6–14 years.
- Non-justiciable (moral obligations).

## Ten memory lines

1. Preamble = identity card; authority = We, the People.
2. Sovereign + Socialist + Secular + Democratic + Republic.
3. Justice + Liberty + Equality + Fraternity = four objectives.
4. FRs are justiciable (Articles 12–35).
5. Art 32 = five writs (heart and soul of Constitution).
6. Art 21 = life and personal liberty; Art 21A = education.
7. Art 24 = no child labour below 14 in hazardous work.
8. DP are non-justiciable (Articles 36–51).
9. Art 51A = Fundamental Duties (42nd Amendment, 1976).
10. Art 44 = Uniform Civil Code (yet to be implemented).
