# Introduction to Constitution — Cheat Sheet

## Preamble keywords

| Keyword | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Sovereign | Internally supreme and free from external control |
| Socialist | Reduce inequality; promote welfare of all |
| Secular | No official religion; all religions equal |
| Democratic | Government elected by and accountable to the people |
| Republic | Head of State (President) is elected, not hereditary |
| Justice | Social, economic, and political |
| Liberty | Freedom of thought, expression, belief, faith, and worship |
| Equality | Equal status and equal opportunity |
| Fraternity | Spirit of brotherhood; dignity of the individual; unity and integrity |

## Fundamental Rights at a glance

| Right | Articles | Key article |
|---|---|---|
| Right to Equality | 14–18 | 14 = equality before law |
| Right to Freedom | 19–22 | 19(1)(a) = speech; 21 = life & liberty |
| Right against Exploitation | 23–24 | 24 = no child labour below 14 |
| Right to Freedom of Religion | 25–28 | 25 = conscience & religion |
| Cultural and Educational Rights | 29–30 | 30 = minority institutions |
| Right to Constitutional Remedies | 32–35 | 32 = five writs |

## The five writs (Article 32)

| Writ | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Habeas corpus | Produce the body; release a person unlawfully detained |
| Mandamus | Command a public authority to perform a duty |
| Prohibition | Stop a lower court/tribunal from exceeding jurisdiction |
| Quo warranto | By what authority; challenge a person holding a public office |
| Certiorari | Quash an order of a lower court/tribunal |

## Fundamental Duties (Article 51A)

1. Abide by the Constitution and respect its ideals and institutions.
2. Cherish and follow the noble ideals of the national struggle for freedom.
3. Uphold and protect the sovereignty, unity, and integrity of India.
4. Defend the country and render national service when called upon.
5. Promote harmony and spirit of common brotherhood; renounce practices derogatory to the dignity of women.
6. Value and preserve the rich heritage of our composite culture.
7. Protect and improve the natural environment and have compassion for living creatures.
8. Develop the scientific temper, humanism, and the spirit of inquiry and reform.
9. Safeguard public property and abjure violence.
10. Strive towards excellence in all spheres of individual and collective activity.
11. Parent/guardian to provide education to child/ward between 6 and 14 years. *(86th Amendment, 2002)*
