# Research Methodology — Complete Study Notes

These exam-oriented notes cover all eleven modules of **Research Methodology**
for MCA. The folder is arranged like a learning dashboard: module notes plus a
"deep dive" article per module, plus quick-revision resources.

The notes have been checked against the 92 source PDFs in
`PdfMaterial/Research Methodology`. The module files include the PDF-aligned
definitions, workflows, formulas, comparisons, assumptions, and exam cautions;
the source PDFs are mainly needed for their original diagrams and extended
illustrations.

Each module follows the three-layer convention used across this repository:

1. **Simple idea:** plain language + a familiar analogy.
2. **Worked example:** a small, concrete application.
3. **Technical meaning:** definitions, checklists, and exam points.

## Running research example

Throughout, we follow one study so the methods feel unified:

> **Research question:** *Does a supervised 30-minute daily exercise plan
> reduce stress levels among full-time MCA students?*

This drives the problem, design, sampling, measurement, and analysis in every
module.

## Module-wise study method

1. Review prerequisites and reconnect the topic to the running stress study.
2. Complete one 10-15-minute study block at a time.
3. Produce the relevant research artifact: question, diagram, sample, instrument, analysis, or paragraph.
4. Attempt the mini quiz and practice ladder before revealing solutions.
5. Mark the module `Mastered` after justifying each methodological choice and one limitation.

## How to use this folder

1. Read the matching module note (start with the "simple idea").
2. Try its self-check questions without looking at the answers.
3. Open the matching article for a deeper explanation.
4. Use the cheat sheet for fast revision of formulas and checklists.
5. Practice the MCQs and important questions.
