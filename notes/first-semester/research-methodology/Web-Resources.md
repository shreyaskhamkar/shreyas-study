# Web Resources for Research Methodology

Links collected per module. Use in this order: easy explanation → official
reference → practice resource.

## Module 1: Introduction & Philosophy

- **Easy second explanation:** [Wikipedia: Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research)
- **Official technical reference:** [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: Scientific Method](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/scientific-method/)
- **Practice resource:** [MIT Writing Center: Research and theses](https://twp.mit.edu/)

## Module 2: Research Problem

- **Easy second explanation:** [University of Minnesota: Formulating a Research Problem](https://www-users.cs.umn.edu/~bhowmick/ResearchProblem.pdf)
- **Official reference:** [Ridsdale & Diodo: Selecting a Research Topic](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4235315/)
- **Practice resource:** Fishbone / 5-whys templates (search "Ishikawa diagram template").

## Module 3: Research Design

- **Easy second explanation:** [Khan Academy: Experimental design](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/statistics-probability/significance-tests)
- **Official reference:** [University of California, Irvine research methods](https://ns5.uci.edu/eduresearch/)
- **Practice resource:** [Statsmodels / SciPy](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/stats.html) for simulation.

## Module 4: Measurement & Scaling

- **Easy second explanation:** [Questionnaire scale types (NOIR)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Level_of_measurement)
- **Official reference:** [Likert scale origin (Rensis Likert, 1932)](https://psycnet.apa.org/pmc/articles/PMC5121368/)
- **Practice resource:** [SPSS tutorials: reliability and validity](https://www.spss-tutorials.com/)

## Module 5: Sampling

- **Easy second explanation:** [Khan Academy: Sampling](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/statistics-probability/sampling-considering-errors)
- **Official reference:** [World Health Organization: Sample size](https://www.who.int/activities/sample-size-determination)
- **Practice resource:** [OpenEpi sample-size calculator](https://www.openepi.com/Menu/OpenEpiMenu.htm)

## Module 6: Data Processing & Analysis

- **Easy second explanation:** [Khan Academy: Statistics and probability](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/statistics-probability)
- **Official reference:** [RDocumentation: base stats](https://stat.ethz.ch/R-manual/R-devel/library/stats/html/stats-package.html)
- **Practice resource:** [GraphPad QuickCalcs](https://www.graphpad.com/quickcalcs/)

## Module 7: Probability

- **Easy second explanation:** [Khan Academy: Probability](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/statistics-probability/probability-library)
- **Official reference:** [MIT 6.041: Probabilistic Systems Analysis](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-041-probabilistic-systems-analysis-and-applied-probability-fall-2010/)
- **Practice resource:** [Stat Trek: probability](https://stattrek.com/probability/probability.aspx)

## Module 8: Statistical Methods

- **Easy second explanation:** [Khan Academy: Statistics](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/statistics-probability/significance-tests)
- **Official reference:** [NIST/SEMATEL e-Handbook of Statistical Methods](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/)
- **Practice resource:** [Social Science Statistics](https://www.socscistat.com/)

## Module 9: Creativity, Writing & IPR
