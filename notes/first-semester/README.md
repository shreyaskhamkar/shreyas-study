## Recommended learning order for these exams

1. Computational Mathematics and Programming from First Principles
2. Processor Architecture and Design and Persistent Data Management
3. Artificial Intelligence
4. Research Methodology

Within a subject, complete modules in numerical order. Study one 10-15-minute
block, attempt its checkpoint, and then continue.
